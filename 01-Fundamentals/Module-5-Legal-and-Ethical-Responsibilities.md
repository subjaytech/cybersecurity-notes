# Module 5 - Legal and Ethical Responsibilities

The final module of Cisco's Introduction to Cybersecurity course focused on the legal and ethical responsibilities of cybersecurity professionals. Rather than concentrating on technical attacks or defence mechanisms, it explored the importance of trust, accountability, and responsible decision-making.

---

---

## Ethical Decision-Making

One of the biggest lessons from this module is that possessing the technical ability to perform an action does not make it ethically or legally acceptable.

Before taking any action, cybersecurity professionals should consider:

- Is it legal?
- Does it comply with organisational policies?
- Does it benefit the organisation and its stakeholders?
- Would it be acceptable if everyone behaved this way?
- Would I be comfortable if this decision became public?

Ethics play a critical role in maintaining trust within organisations and protecting users.

---

## Example Scenario

The course presented a scenario where a penetration tester discovers a mistake made by a colleague.

Choosing whether to report or conceal the mistake is more than a legal decision. It is a question of professional integrity and accountability. Reporting the issue helps reduce organisational risk and strengthens security practices.
