### Best Practices

- Use strong passphrases.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Keep software updated.
- Secure wireless networks.
- Review privacy settings regularly.

## IDS vs IPS

IDS

- Detects attacks
- Generates alerts

IPS

- Detects attacks
- Blocks malicious traffic
