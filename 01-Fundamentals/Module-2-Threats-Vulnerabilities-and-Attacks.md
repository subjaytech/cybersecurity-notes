# Module 2 - Threats, Vulnerabilities and Attacks

## Common Malware

- Virus
- Worm
- Trojan
- Spyware
- Adware
- Ransomware

---

## Social Engineering

Social engineering targets people instead of systems.

Examples include:

- Phishing
- Pretexting
- Baiting

---

## Password Attacks

Common techniques:

- Brute force
- Dictionary attack
- Credential stuffing

---

## Network Attacks

- DoS
- DDoS
- On-path attacks

---

## Vulnerability vs Exploit

A vulnerability is a weakness.

An exploit is the method used to abuse that weakness.

---

## Hardware Vulnerabilities

Examples include:

- Meltdown
- Spectre

---

## Cryptojacking

Cryptojacking occurs when attackers secretly use another person's computing resources to mine cryptocurrency.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Every exploit begins with a vulnerability.
- Regular software updates reduce risk.
- Security awareness is as important as technical controls.
